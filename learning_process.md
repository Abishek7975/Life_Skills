# Learning Process

**Question 1**

- The Feynman technique is a method for understanding a subject in depth. It states that you haven't completely understood a topic unless you can explain it to a five-year-old child.

**Question 2**
- The most interesting idea was that of the Pomodoro technique. This technique suggests that working in a slot of 25 minutes and then taking a 5-minute break can significantly help in the learning process.

**Question 3**
- In Active mode, you turn off all distractions and focus only on the task at hand. In diffused mode, you just take a break from your work and relax. Observe the ideas, thoughts you are coming up with.

**Question 4**
To learn a new skill-
1. Deconstruct that skill into smaller, accomplishable skills or goals.
2. Learn just enough to self-correct
3. Remove barriers to practicing the skill.
4. Spend 20 hours practicing (in smaller chunks).

**Question 5**
To improve my learning process, I will:
1. Turn off notifications from non-essential apps.
2. Utilise the Pomodoro technique.
3. Write or explain in my own words the concept I am trying to learn.
4. Make the process of learning fun and easy.
