# Listening and Active Communication

**Question 1**

Steps/strategies to do Active Listening- 
1. Avoid getting distracted by your thoughts
2. Try not to interrupt when someone is talking.
3. Let them finish and then respond.
4. Use Door Openers.
5. Use body language to show you're interested in the conversation.
6. Make notes, if appropriate.

**Question 2**

According to Fisher's model, the key points of Reflective Listening are - 
1. Convey what the other person is trying to explain in your own words.
2. Make notes during the conversation. Share this with others to make sure you are on the same page.
3. Give sound feedback on the topic to further expand and understand the topic fully.
